🏆 28-Day Beginner Calisthenics Quest

No equipment • 10–20 min daily • Check off each day as you complete it.

Week 1: Learn Foundational Bodyweight Movements

Day 1: 3×8 Incline Push-ups, 3×10 Squats, 3×20s Plank
Day 2: 2×8 Lunges/leg, 3×10 Glute Bridges, 2×10 Dead Bugs
Day 3: Recovery + Walk
Day 4: 3×10 Incline Push-ups, 3×12 Squats, 3×25s Plank
Day 5: 3×8 Lunges/leg, 3×12 Glute Bridges, Superman Holds
Day 6: Circuit ×2
Day 7: Recovery

Week 2: Build Control & Core Stability

Day 8–14: Progress core, knee push-ups, stability work

Week 3: Increase Strength & Endurance

Day 15–21: More reps, longer planks, 3-round circuits

Week 4: Move With Power & Confidence

Day 22–28: Full-body circuits, fitness test, victory workout

🎮 Level-Up Rules

  • Complete a day = +10 XP
  • Complete a week = +100 XP
  • No missed workouts in a week = Bonus +50 XP
  • Beat your Day 27 fitness test = Boss Defeated 🏆
